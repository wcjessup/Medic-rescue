HOW TO PLAY

      

        
You are a paramedic. Dispatch gives you almost nothing. Everything else you have to find out yourself.

        
    
          
  • Notepad (left) is your running chart. Nothing appears there unless you go get it.
    • 
          
  • Monitor shows a live rhythm once you attach leads, but numbers stay blank until you actually check each vital.
    • 
          
  • Click the patient to examine body regions. Choose HISTORY to ask questions, EXAM for physical findings, VITALS to take readings, KIT to give treatment, PROCEDURES for access/airway/monitor/defib.
    • 
          
  • The kit bag is unforgiving. You must pick the exact drug, concentration, dose and route. IV/IO drugs are locked out until you've established access. Wrong choices can hurt the patient.
    • 
          
  • When you're ready, open DIAGNOSIS to commit to a provisional diagnosis and a disposition (stay home, GP referral, transport priority, hospital type, or ceasing resuscitation). That ends the case and you're graded.
